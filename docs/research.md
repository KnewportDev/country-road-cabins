## Facts to confirm before a commercial launch

- Safari Sun's collection summary says four guests, while its detailed page describes two in the king bed plus one on the futon. The concept uses the more conservative three-person capacity. Confirm with the owner.
- Love Shack's prose and amenity list disagree on king versus queen bed. The concept does not specify bed size.
- An older information PDF says check-in after 4 p.m.; the current contact page says 3 p.m. The concept omits check-in time.
- Glamping season dates differ between the homepage and accommodation pages. The concept does not promise year-round glamping.
- Prices, availability, discounts and cancellation terms remain with the existing reservation system.

## Assets and identity

`asset-sources.json` records image URLs retrieved from the official site and its stylesheet. Photos were downloaded, resized and encoded as WebP. The hero is Tuscany Treehouse. No generated property images are used.

The existing logo is a colorful cabin illustration with an arched wordmark. Its site uses green (`#005826`, `#87BB9D`) and Roboto/Roboto Slab. This proposal explores a quieter green-and-cream palette, Instrument Serif, DM Sans and a simple tree wordmark. The proposed mark is a design study, not an adopted business logo.

The business and image owners retain rights in photography and trademarks. The source-code license does not grant rights to these assets. Obtain approval for any permanent commercial replacement.
